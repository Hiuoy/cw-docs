# Zombie types

> The AI types of the Zombies zones: 57 in all, 16 of them known by name.

## How a map gets its zombies

- An **AI type** is an asset: the body, the behaviour and the animations of one kind of enemy.
- A level spawns them from **spawner templates**: entities whose class is `actor_` and the AI type's name.
- A mapkit map keeps Die Maschine's templates. You do not pick the zombie type per spawner yet: the round logic of the shared Zombies scripts decides what comes.
- A map that no longer loads Die Maschine's zones has only the common zone's AI types. cwlink then swaps a template whose type would be missing for the closest common one.
