## How the names are built

A player weapon is `<class>_<role>_t9`. The same name with `_upgraded` is its Pack-a-Punched form, going by the name.

| Starts with | What | Names |
|---|---|---|
| `ar_` | Assault rifles | 23 |
| `smg_` | Submachine guns | 24 |
| `tr_` | Tactical rifles | 10 |

| `eq_` | Equipment | 10 |

74 names end in `_t9` and are not upgraded forms: the weapons a player holds. The rest are upgraded forms, equipment, field upgrades, scorestreaks, perk bottles and the weapons the zombies and bosses use.
