# Models

> The names of 4420 game models a prop, a door or a wall buy can use. They come from Die Maschine's zone and the common Zombies zone.

## Using a name

- In the editor, a model field has a **Pick** button that shows these models with a picture. See [Props and models](/guide/mapping/props-models.md).
- Typed by hand, a model is its name, or `#` and its 16-digit hash for a model that has no name here.
- About 7 in 100 models have no known name. The picker lists them by hash.
- A name that starts with `*` is a piece of Die Maschine's own world, cut by the game's map compiler.
